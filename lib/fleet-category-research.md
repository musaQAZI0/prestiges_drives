# Vehicle category review

Reviewed 7 November 2026. MUVs is the customer-facing label for the MPV data category.

- Changan markets Karvaan as an MPV: https://changan.com.pk/karvaan-mpv/
  Place it with Suzuki APV in MUVs, rather than the Hiace category.
- Toyota lists Hilux/Revo, Hiace and Coaster as distinct vehicle lines:
  https://toyota-indus.com/showroom/
  Revo/Vigo are pickups; retain their own category after the six requested categories.
- Toyota Hiace specifications cover standard/high-roof passenger van variants:
  https://toyota-indus.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Hiace-Spec-Sheet-2.pdf
  Hiace and Grand Cabin entries share the Hiace filter; Coaster entries use Buses.
- Suzuki APV is classified as MPV in Suzuki's corporate offering document:
  https://links.sgx.com/FileOpen/SUZUKI%20MOTOR%20CORPORATION%20-%20Final%20Offering%20Circular.ashx?App=Prospectus&FileID=28344
- Honda BR-V remains an SUV/crossover, despite seven seats:
  https://www.hondadrivein.com/br-v.html

Remaining existing sedan, hatchback and SUV classifications were retained.
